# Transmog Studio - how to

1. Visit your bank once per character so bank items are remembered.
2. Type `/ts` to open it. It suggests an outfit from gear you own, shown on a model.
3. **Like** / **Dislike** / **Skip**: it learns your taste. Liked looks are also saved into DressMe.
4. **Click a slot** to lock that piece while the rest changes.
5. **Favorites** (top button): browse liked outfits with Prev / Next, or Remove one.

`/ts forget` wipes what it learned about your taste.
