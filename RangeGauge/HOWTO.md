# Range Gauge - how to

- **Setup:** drag the "Range lights - drag me" box where you want it, then `/rg lock`.
- **Rows:** your target (T), focus (F), your 5-man group (P1-P4) and arena enemies (A1-A5).
- **Rule:** enemies: bright = in your attack range, dark = out. Allies: green = you can heal them,
  gold = edge of range, red = out of range (and their name fades).
- **Click + flash** when someone crosses your range line.
- **Mouseover macros** (`/cast [@mouseover,help] Flash Heal`) work on the rows: hover a row and
  press your key. Left click targets. Click-cast addons (Clique) work too.
- **In combat** rows stay in place (no re-sorting) so you don't misclick; they tidy up after.

| Command | Does |
|---|---|
| `/rg lock` / `/rg unlock` | lock or move the display |
| `/rg bar` / `/rg lights` | switch view |
| `/rg party` | hide/show group rows |
| `/rg mute` / `/rg unmute` | click sound off/on |
| `/rg reset` | move back to the default spot |
